Changelog

Every change to tarang, newest first. Releases are git tags (all tags, GitHub releases); the source of this page is CHANGELOG.md.

Unreleased

  • The website, closer to Vayu's own: the tool's screenshot blurred behind the page, a centred hero with the logo and the verse, the page you're on as the white pill, glass "On this page" and footer panels, releases as cards. No glow behind the logo, no coloured line beside the verse.
  • Spatial look: rounder, borderless glass cards for the live view.
  • The website has Vayu's Spatial look: graphite glass, a floating navigation bar, near-white for links and the main button, and the tool's own colour only as its signature (the logo's glow, the shloka's line, the release dots). No more all-caps labels.

v0.5.02026-11-09Release notes

  • Live speeds on the right. With the network you're on picked (it is when the panel opens), the right side is a live view: download and upload as big numbers (sky and lavender) with their peak and average, the last minute as a graph with the peaks marked, data received and sent, how long you've been connected and since when (NetworkManager's log), the signal with its own line and dBm, and every fact at a glance: internet (Online, Limited, Sign in, No internet, from NetworkManager), security, band and channel, IP, router, link rate both ways (iw, when it's there), DNS, access point, IPv6. It moves once a second, only while the panel is open, and reads the card's own counters (/sys/class/net/<card>/statistics, /proc/net/wireless): nothing is sent to measure it.
  • The list was empty while you were connected ("No networks found yet") when the network you're on was the only one in range: it was left out of the list. It's now the first row, under Connected.
  • Another network picked: its signal compared with the one you're on ("17 points weaker than Home Fiber 5G"), and its facts in the same tidy boxes (security, band, frequency, top speed, saved, access point, metered).
  • The hotspot's traffic, live: on the Hotspot tab, while it's on, the right side shows what its devices send and get (the last minute, totals) above the picked device or setting.
  • Wi‑Fi off is a row: Enter on it turns Wi‑Fi on, and the right side says what's going on (offline, or online over Ethernet).
  • A tighter card at the top: a smaller ring, signal and band, and your download and upload right now (so they're in sight with the right side hidden, Space u p); the card's buttons keep their keys in their tooltips and the footer, and the hotspot's QR code is a fixed size, so the panel never grows wider than its own size.
  • New unit tests (tests/test_speed.py, run in CI) for the speeds, units, the graph's scale and the sampler.

v0.4.02026-11-09Release notes

  • Fixed: while typing a password or a hotspot name, every Ctrl chord but Ctrl+S was swallowed, so Ctrl+V couldn't paste a password and Ctrl+Backspace / Ctrl+A didn't edit; the box's own editing chords work again (Tab and the tool's Ctrl keys stay swallowed).
  • Vim keys (Vyom 0.7): tarang opens in NORMAL mode, where letters are keys and typing changes nothing; / (or i) types a filter, Esc goes back. Space is the leader, with a which-key strip; ? lists every key and Space Space is a palette of every action. H / L switch tabs.
  • A password or a hotspot field types in INSERT mode by itself: Enter submits, Esc cancels it, Ctrl+S shows it. While you type one, tabs and other keys don't switch away from it any more.
  • New keys: Space p p / p h turn Wi-Fi / the hotspot on or off, Space c d disconnects, Space c q shares the network as a QR code (on the Hotspot tab: shows or hides its QR code), Space u s shows the passwords, Space y p / y s copy the password / the name, r rescans, e edits a hotspot setting.
  • Forget asks with one question: dd (or Space d d) asks "Forget …?", y forgets and any other key keeps it, instead of pressing Ctrl+F twice (Ctrl+F now says it moved). Ctrl+D scrolls half a page down, as in every vim tool; Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+Q, Ctrl+S and Ctrl+R still work.
  • Keys are data (tarang/vim.py); scripts/keybinds.py writes the # keybind: lines and the README table from it (--check in CI).
  • Needs Vyom 0.7.0.

v0.3.22026-11-08Release notes

  • The footer's keys for Networks and Hotspot come from Vyom's tab_hints() (Vyom 0.6.2) instead of tarang rewiring the footer; a status message such as "not connected: nothing to share" now shows in full.

v0.3.12026-11-07Release notes

  • The signal meter no longer makes GTK warn about its size (its bar had a negative width).

v0.3.02026-11-07Release notes

  • A new look (Vyom 0.6.0's status strip). The network you're on is a card at the top: signal ring, link speed, band and channel, security, your address, and Share / Disconnect buttons with their keys on them. When you're not connected it says why (Wi-Fi off, on Ethernet, NetworkManager not running, no Wi-Fi card) instead of an empty list.
  • Signal is bars and a word (Excellent, Good, Fair, Weak) instead of a bare percentage; saved networks have a star; open networks a yellow "Open" chip, WEP/WPA1 a red one; Saved and Nearby sections with counts. The top bar shows when it's scanning and how long ago it last scanned.
  • Connecting shows its steps (Associating, Authenticating, Getting an address) as NetworkManager goes through them, and a failure says why in red: wrong password (it asks again right away), out of range, no address from the router, a WPA Enterprise network, a password that's too short. The panel stays open, so you see it connect.
  • The preview has a signal meter with what it means in plain words, security as a chip (with a warning on open and weak networks), band and channel, speed, metered, BSSID.
  • Forget asks first: press Ctrl+F twice (it can't be undone).
  • New: Ctrl+Q shares the network you're on as a QR code phones scan to join (needs qrencode; without it, the password is shown); Ctrl+S shows or hides a password.
  • The Hotspot tab: a QR code to join, name, password (hidden until Ctrl+S), band and what it shares, the connected devices, Ctrl+T (or the switch) to turn it on or off, and name, password and band as settings that are read-only until you press Enter on one. Errors from the hotspot command show in red with its reason.
  • The on/off switch is a pill that says On or Off. Needs Vyom 0.6.0.
  • Every corner is rounded: the website's callouts.
  • A logo and a shloka at the top of the README and the website: every Vayu tool has them now.
  • Screenshots in the README and on the website.

v0.1.02026-10-19Release notes

  • First release, as its own repository: wi‑Fi and the hotspot. Replaces networkmanager_dmenu (wifi-picker.py) in Vayu.
  • Networks with live signal bars (rescanned every few seconds while open): the one you're on, then saved ones, then nearby.
  • Enter connects; a secured network you haven't joined asks for its password right in the search box.
  • Disconnect, forget, rescan, and a Wi‑Fi on/off switch; Ethernet shows when it's what you're on.
  • The preview: signal, security, band, channel, BSSID, your address.
  • A Hotspot tab (with Vayu's vayu-hotspot, or any command with its status --json / on / off interface): on/off, name and password, a QR code phones scan to join, connected devices.
